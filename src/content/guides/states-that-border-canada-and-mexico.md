<div class="key-takeaway"><p><strong>Quick answer:</strong> Thirteen states border Canada: Alaska, Washington, Idaho, Montana, North Dakota, Minnesota, Michigan, Ohio, Pennsylvania, New York, Vermont, New Hampshire and Maine. Four border Mexico: California, Arizona, New Mexico and Texas.</p></div>

The northern and southern borders are two of the quickest things to learn on the US map, and they help you place the states around them. Here's how each border runs, west to east.

The three people forget are **Ohio**, **Pennsylvania** and **Michigan**, because their borders with Canada run through water. Ohio's and Pennsylvania's lie in the middle of Lake Erie.

Between Washington and Minnesota, much of the border follows the 49th parallel, a straight line of latitude set by treaties in 1818 and 1846. That's why the border looks ruler-drawn across the plains.

Minnesota also has the Northwest Angle, a small piece of land north of the 49th parallel that can be reached by land only through Canada. It's the northernmost point of the lower 48 states.

In Texas, the border follows the Rio Grande from El Paso all the way to the Gulf of Mexico. West of El Paso, the border runs over land through New Mexico, Arizona and California.

## Quick facts

- **Longest land border with Canada:** Alaska's.
- **States that border both countries:** none.
- **The US–Canada border** is the longest international border in the world.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many US states border Canada?

Thirteen, including Ohio and Pennsylvania, whose borders with Canada run through Lake Erie.

### How many states border Mexico?

Four: California, Arizona, New Mexico and Texas.

### Which state has the longest border with Canada?

Alaska, which borders the Yukon and British Columbia.

### Does any state border both Canada and Mexico?

No. No state touches both borders.
