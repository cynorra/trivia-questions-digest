<div class="key-takeaway"><p><strong>Quick answer:</strong> World War II began on September 1, 1939, when Germany invaded Poland, and ended in Europe on May 8, 1945 (VE Day). It ended in the Pacific when Japan announced its surrender on August 15, 1945, and signed it aboard USS Missouri on September 2, 1945. The United States entered the war after the attack on Pearl Harbor on December 7, 1941.</p></div>

World War II questions are a staple of history rounds because the key dates are well documented and easy to anchor. This timeline sticks to the events that come up most often. When you're done, test yourself with our [history trivia](/history-trivia/).

## The war year by year

### 1939

- **September 1:** Germany invades Poland.
- **September 3:** Britain and France declare war on Germany.
- **September 17:** The Soviet Union invades Poland from the east, under its secret pact with Germany.

### 1940

- **April–May:** Germany invades Denmark and Norway, then on **May 10** the Netherlands, Belgium, Luxembourg and France. Winston Churchill becomes British prime minister the same day.
- **May 26 – June 4:** More than 330,000 Allied troops are evacuated from Dunkirk.
- **June 22:** France signs an armistice with Germany.
- **July–October:** The Battle of Britain, fought in the air. Germany fails to gain control of the skies over Britain.

### 1941

- **June 22:** Germany invades the Soviet Union (Operation Barbarossa).
- **December 7:** Japan attacks the US naval base at Pearl Harbor, Hawaii.
- **December 8:** The United States declares war on Japan.
- **December 11:** Germany and Italy declare war on the United States.

### 1942

- **June 4–7:** The Battle of Midway, a decisive US naval victory over Japan in the Pacific.
- **August:** The Battle of Stalingrad begins. It ends with a German surrender in **February 1943**.
- **October–November:** The Second Battle of El Alamein in Egypt turns the North African campaign toward the Allies.

### 1943

- **July 10:** Allied forces land in Sicily. Italy surrenders in September, though fighting there continues against German forces.

### 1944

- **June 6:** D-Day. Allied forces land in Normandy, France, in Operation Overlord.
- **August 25:** Paris is liberated.
- **December 16:** Germany launches its last major offensive in the west, the Battle of the Bulge.

### 1945

- **February:** Roosevelt, Churchill and Stalin meet at Yalta. US Marines land on Iwo Jima.
- **April 12:** President Franklin D. Roosevelt dies; Harry S. Truman becomes president.
- **April 30:** Adolf Hitler dies in Berlin.
- **May 8:** VE Day (Victory in Europe), after Germany's unconditional surrender.
- **August 6 and 9:** The United States drops atomic bombs on Hiroshima and Nagasaki.
- **August 15:** Japan announces its surrender.
- **September 2:** Japan formally signs the surrender aboard USS Missouri in Tokyo Bay.

## Dates people mix up

- **VE Day vs. VJ Day.** VE Day is May 8, 1945. VJ Day is celebrated on August 15 in Britain and many other countries, and on September 2 in the United States, the day of the formal signing.
- **When the war started for the US.** The war began in 1939, but the United States entered only in December 1941.
- **D-Day.** "D-Day" is a military term for the start of any operation; in trivia it almost always means June 6, 1944.

## Frequently asked questions

### When did World War II start and end?

It started on September 1, 1939, with Germany's invasion of Poland. It ended in Europe on May 8, 1945, and in the Pacific with Japan's formal surrender on September 2, 1945.

### Why did the United States enter World War II?

Japan attacked Pearl Harbor on December 7, 1941. The US declared war on Japan the next day, and Germany and Italy declared war on the US on December 11.

### What were the five beaches of D-Day?

Utah and Omaha (assigned to US forces), Gold and Sword (British) and Juno (Canadian).

### What does VE Day stand for?

Victory in Europe Day, May 8, 1945, when Germany's unconditional surrender took effect.
