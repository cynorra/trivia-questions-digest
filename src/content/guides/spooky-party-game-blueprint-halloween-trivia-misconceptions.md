In truth, the holiday traces its roots back thousands of years to the ancient Celtic festival of Samhain. Observed around November 1, Samhain marked the end of the harvest season and the beginning of winter. By contrast, the Celtic festival of Beltane was celebrated in May to mark the beginning of summer.</p><p>The name Halloween itself comes from All Hallows' Eve, which is the evening before All Saints' Day on November 1.
