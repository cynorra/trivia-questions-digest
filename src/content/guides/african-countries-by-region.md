<div class="key-takeaway"><p><strong>Quick answer:</strong> Africa has 54 countries, more than any other continent. Learn them in five regions (North, West, Central, East and Southern Africa) and add the six island nations at the end. The two Congos and the three Guineas cause the most mistakes.</p></div>

An African countries quiz is the biggest single-continent challenge in geography: 54 countries, several of them small and many with borders drawn in straight lines across deserts. Breaking the continent into regions turns it into five much smaller puzzles.

The regions below follow common usage, similar to the groupings used by the United Nations. Some countries are placed differently by different sources; what matters for learning is a consistent grouping.

## North Africa (7)

**Morocco**, **Algeria**, **Tunisia**, **Libya**, **Egypt** and **Sudan** run along the north and east, with **Mauritania** on the Atlantic side. Algeria is the largest country in Africa. Egypt is easy: its border with Libya and Sudan is made of straight lines, and the Nile runs through it from south to north.

## West Africa (15)

This is the hardest region. Work along the coast from northwest to southeast:

1. **Senegal**, with **The Gambia** as a thin strip almost entirely inside it.
2. **Guinea-Bissau** and **Guinea**.
3. **Sierra Leone** and **Liberia**.
4. **Côte d'Ivoire** and **Ghana**.
5. **Togo** and **Benin**, two narrow countries side by side.
6. **Nigeria**, the most populous country in Africa.

Inland are **Mali**, **Burkina Faso** and **Niger**, and offshore is the island nation of **Cabo Verde**.

## Central Africa (9)

The **Democratic Republic of the Congo** (DR Congo) is the giant in the middle, the second-largest country in Africa. To its west is the much smaller **Republic of the Congo**; the two capitals, Kinshasa and Brazzaville, face each other across the Congo River.

Around them: **Cameroon**, **Central African Republic**, **Chad**, **Gabon**, **Equatorial Guinea**, **Angola** to the south, and the island nation of **São Tomé and Príncipe**. Northern Chad reaches deep into the Sahara.

## East Africa (14)

From the Horn of Africa southward: **Eritrea**, **Djibouti**, **Ethiopia** and **Somalia**, then **South Sudan**, **Uganda**, **Kenya**, **Rwanda**, **Burundi** and **Tanzania**. Rwanda and Burundi are the small pair between DR Congo and Tanzania; Rwanda is the northern one.

Offshore in the Indian Ocean are **Madagascar**, **Comoros**, **Mauritius** and **Seychelles**.

## Southern Africa (9)

**South Africa** anchors the south. **Lesotho** sits entirely inside it, and **Eswatini** is nearly surrounded, also bordering **Mozambique**. To the north are **Namibia**, **Botswana**, **Zimbabwe**, **Zambia** and **Malawi**. Malawi is the long, thin country along Lake Malawi.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many countries are in Africa?

Fifty-four, all members of the United Nations.

### What is the largest country in Africa?

Algeria, by area. Nigeria has the largest population.

### What is the smallest country in Africa?

Seychelles, an island nation in the Indian Ocean, by both area and population. On the mainland, The Gambia is the smallest.

### Which part of Africa is hardest to learn?

West Africa, where 15 countries share the coast and the Sahel, several of them small and similar in shape.
