A Christmas trivia game is one of the easiest holiday activities to run: nearly everyone knows the songs and films, the questions start conversations, and it works for an office party, a family gathering or a holiday pub quiz. Here's a ready plan, with questions you can take straight from our [Christmas trivia](/christmas-trivia/) set.

## The five rounds

### Round 1: Christmas songs

Start with songs everyone has heard a thousand times. Our set has the first gift in "The Twelve Days of Christmas," the best-selling single of all time (Bing Crosby's "White Christmas," according to Guinness World Records), and what "O Tannenbaum" means. For a music twist, play the first few seconds of a carol and ask for the title.

### Round 2: Christmas movies

Home Alone, Elf, It's a Wonderful Life, A Christmas Story and How the Grinch Stole Christmas. Ask for character names, famous lines ("You'll shoot your eye out") and details such as the name of the Grinch's dog (Max).

### Round 3: Traditions around the world

Where the Christmas tree custom came from (Germany), which country sends London's Trafalgar Square tree every year (Norway, since 1947), where poinsettias are native (Mexico), and what Boxing Day is. Our guide to [Christmas traditions around the world](/guides/christmas-traditions-around-the-world/) has plenty more.

### Round 4: The picture round

Print a sheet of 12 images: close-ups of famous Christmas film scenes (drawn or described, if you can't use stills), Christmas cookies from different countries, or ornaments shaped like things teams must name. Hand it out at the start and collect it at the break.

### Round 5: Christmas trivia for adults (double points)

Finish with the harder history: the year Christmas became a US federal holiday (1870), who is usually credited with writing "'Twas the Night Before Christmas" (Clement Clarke Moore), and which company's 1930s ads helped fix Santa's modern look (Coca-Cola). Worth double points, this round keeps everyone in the race.

## Tiebreakers

- How many gifts are given in total in "The Twelve Days of Christmas"? **364**
- In what year did Christmas become a US federal holiday? **1870**
- In what year did Oslo first send London its Christmas tree? **1947**

## Running it at work

At an office party, keep it to three rounds and 30 minutes. Mix departments in each team, and use multiple choice so nobody feels caught out. A box of chocolates or a silly trophy is plenty as a prize. For general advice on timing and scoring, see [how to host a trivia night](/guides/how-to-host-a-trivia-night/).

## Running it with family

With mixed ages, pair children with adults, read the movie round first, and let younger players answer the easy song questions aloud for a bonus point. Keep a few general questions from [today's quiz](/daily-trivia/) in reserve for anyone who's had enough of Christmas.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are good Christmas trivia categories?

Christmas songs, Christmas movies, traditions around the world, a picture round, and a harder history round. Five rounds of eight questions is a comfortable length.

### How do you make Christmas trivia fun for adults?

Add a hard history round worth double points, use real tiebreakers, and keep the pace quick. Questions with surprising answers, like the 1870 federal holiday date, work best.

### How long should a Christmas trivia game be?

About 60 to 90 minutes for a party, or around 30 minutes for an office lunch with three short rounds.

### Can kids play Christmas trivia with adults?

Yes. Put children on teams with adults, start with movies and songs, and give younger players a bonus point for answering aloud.
