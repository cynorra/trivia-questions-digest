Asia is easiest to learn from its giants outward. Saudi Arabia, Kazakhstan, India, China and Indonesia each anchor a region, and everything else is a neighbor you place relative to one of them. Asia stretches from Turkey to Japan and holds 47 countries in our [Asia map quiz](/asia-map-quiz/); the Gulf states and Central Asia's five "-stans" need the most practice.

Russia spans the north of the continent but is asked in the Europe quiz; it's drawn on the Asia map for context. Totals vary between sources depending on whether Russia, Turkey, Cyprus and the Caucasus countries are placed in Asia or Europe.

## Anchor 1: Saudi Arabia and the Middle East (15)

**Saudi Arabia** is the largest country in the Middle East. On the Arabian Peninsula, **Yemen** and **Oman** lie to its south and east. Along the Persian Gulf coast, from north to south, are **Kuwait**, **Bahrain** (an island), **Qatar** (a peninsula) and the **United Arab Emirates**; learn them as a sequence, then Oman around the corner.

To the north, **Turkey** anchors the northwest. South of it are **Syria**, **Lebanon**, **Israel**, **Palestine** and **Jordan** along the Mediterranean side, with **Iraq** and **Iran** to the east. Cyprus, though in the eastern Mediterranean, is grouped with Europe on this site.

## Between two seas: the Caucasus (3)

**Georgia** in the north, **Armenia** in the southwest and **Azerbaijan** in the east, along the Caspian coast. Azerbaijan's Nakhchivan exclave sits separately on the other side of Armenia.

## Anchor 2: Kazakhstan and its four neighbors (5)

**Kazakhstan** is by far the largest, and the largest landlocked country in the world. The other four "-stans" fit around it:

1. **Uzbekistan**, in the middle, one of only two doubly landlocked countries.
2. **Turkmenistan**, to the southwest, on the Caspian Sea.
3. **Kyrgyzstan**, small and mountainous, to the east.
4. **Tajikistan**, south of Kyrgyzstan, bordering Afghanistan and China.

## Anchor 3: India and its neighbors (8)

**India** dominates, with **Pakistan** to the west and **Bangladesh** to the east. **Nepal** and **Bhutan** sit in the Himalayas between India and China; Nepal is the larger, western one. **Afghanistan** borders Pakistan and Iran. Off the coast are **Sri Lanka** and the **Maldives**, a chain of low coral islands.

## Anchor 4: China and East Asia (5)

**China**, **Mongolia**, **North Korea**, **South Korea** and **Japan**. Mongolia is landlocked between China and Russia. Taiwan is drawn on the map but not asked, following the rule that the quiz covers UN member and observer states.

## Anchor 5: Indonesia and Southeast Asia (11)

On the mainland: **Myanmar**, **Thailand**, **Laos**, **Cambodia** and **Vietnam**. Laos is the only landlocked country in Southeast Asia. The Malay Peninsula and islands hold **Malaysia**, **Singapore**, **Brunei**, **Indonesia**, the **Philippines** and **Timor-Leste**. Malaysia is split between the peninsula and northern Borneo, an island it shares with Brunei and Indonesia.

## The landlocked twelve

Twelve countries in this quiz have no ocean coast: Afghanistan, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Bhutan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Laos, Mongolia, Nepal, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan and Uzbekistan. Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan and Turkmenistan border the Caspian Sea, but because it is a lake with no outlet to the ocean, they still count as landlocked. China is the largest country entirely in Asia; Russia is larger but spans Europe and Asia.
