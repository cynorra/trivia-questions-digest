A common memory aid for the order is to remember the two ends: Asia is the biggest, Australia the smallest, and the Americas sit in the middle with Africa.

## Where one continent ends and another begins

- **Europe and Asia** share one landmass. The usual dividing line runs along the Ural Mountains, the Ural River, the Caspian Sea and the Caucasus, then through the Black Sea and the Turkish straits. That's why Russia and Turkey span two continents.
- **Africa and Asia** meet at the Isthmus of Suez, crossed by the Suez Canal. Egypt's Sinai Peninsula lies on the Asian side.
- **North and South America** meet at the Isthmus of Panama. Panama is usually counted as part of North America.
- **Australia** is sometimes called a continent and sometimes grouped with the Pacific islands as **Oceania**, which is how our map quizzes group it.

## Why some people count six continents

There's no single world standard. Many schools in English-speaking countries teach seven continents. In much of Latin America and parts of Europe, students learn that **America** is one continent, giving six. Others join **Europe and Asia** into **Eurasia**. The differences are about tradition, not geography: the land is the same either way.

## The five oceans by size

1. **Pacific Ocean:** the largest and deepest, covering more area than all of Earth's land combined.
2. **Atlantic Ocean:** between the Americas and Europe and Africa.
3. **Indian Ocean:** bounded by Africa, Asia and Australia.
4. **Southern Ocean:** the waters around Antarctica. The US National Geographic Society began recognizing it as the fifth ocean in 2021.
5. **Arctic Ocean:** the smallest and shallowest, around the North Pole.

## How to remember them on the map

- Picture the Pacific as the ocean you cross from the Americas to Asia and Australia.
- The Atlantic is the narrower ocean you cross from the Americas to Europe and Africa.
- The Indian Ocean is the one south of India, the only ocean named after a country.
- The Southern and Arctic oceans are at the bottom and top of the world.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are the 7 continents in order of size?

Asia, Africa, North America, South America, Antarctica, Europe and Australia.

### What are the 5 oceans?

The Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Southern and Arctic oceans, from largest to smallest.

### Which continent has the most countries?

Africa, with 54.

### Is Europe really a separate continent from Asia?

Geologically they're one landmass. They're counted separately mainly for historical and cultural reasons, with the Ural Mountains as the traditional dividing line.
