Learn the pairs together and remember only the difference, such as "Ireland starts with green at the flagpole, Côte d'Ivoire starts with orange."</p></div>

Many flags share colors on purpose. The pan-African colors (green, yellow and red), the pan-Arab colors (red, white, black and green) and the red, white and blue of many European tricolors each appear on dozens of flags. That's why a guess-the-flag quiz feels easy for the first twenty and hard for the rest.

The fix isn't to memorize every flag separately. It's to learn the confusable ones in pairs and focus on what's different.

## Families of similar flags

### The Nordic crosses

Denmark, Norway, Sweden, Finland and Iceland all use a cross shifted toward the flagpole.

- **Denmark:** white cross on red.
- **Norway:** blue cross outlined in white, on red.
- **Sweden:** yellow cross on blue.
- **Finland:** blue cross on white.
- **Iceland:** red cross outlined in white, on blue.

### Australia and New Zealand

Both show the British Union Jack in the corner and the stars of the Southern Cross. Australia's flag adds a large seven-pointed star beneath the Union Jack and uses white stars; New Zealand's has four red stars outlined in white.

### Pan-Slavic tricolors

Russia, Slovakia, Slovenia and Serbia all use white, blue and red stripes. Russia's is plain. Slovakia, Slovenia and Serbia each add a coat of arms toward the flagpole, and Serbia's stripes run red, blue, white from the top, the reverse of Russia's.

### Pan-Arab colors

Jordan, Palestine, Sudan, Kuwait and the United Arab Emirates use red, white, black and green. Look at the shape at the flagpole: a red triangle for Jordan (with a small white star) and Palestine, a green triangle for Sudan, a black trapezoid for Kuwait, and a red vertical band for the UAE.

## One flag that breaks every rule

Nepal's flag is the only national flag that isn't a rectangle. It's two stacked triangles, and you'll never confuse it with anything.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which two flags are the most alike?

Chad and Romania. Their stripes are the same colors in the same order, and the only difference is a slightly darker blue on Chad's flag.

### Why do so many flags use the same colors?

Many countries chose colors to show shared history or identity, such as the pan-African and pan-Arab colors, or adopted tricolors modeled on older European flags.

### What's the fastest way to learn world flags?

Learn them by continent, then practice the look-alike pairs side by side and remember only the detail that differs.
