<div class="key-takeaway"><p><strong>Quick answer:</strong> Split Asia into six regions: the Middle East, the Caucasus, Central Asia, South Asia, East Asia and Southeast Asia. Start each with its biggest country (Saudi Arabia, Kazakhstan, India, China, Indonesia) and place the neighbors around it. The Gulf states and Central Asia's five "-stans" need the most practice.</p></div>

Asia stretches from Turkey to Japan and holds 47 countries in our [Asia map quiz](/asia-map-quiz/). Russia spans the north of the continent but is asked in the Europe quiz; it's drawn on the Asia map for context.

## The Middle East (15)

**Turkey** anchors the northwest. South of it are **Syria**, **Lebanon**, **Israel**, **Palestine** and **Jordan** along the Mediterranean side, with **Iraq** and **Iran** to the east.

The Arabian Peninsula holds **Saudi Arabia**, the largest country in the Middle East, with **Yemen** and **Oman** to its south and east. Along the Persian Gulf coast, from north to south, are **Kuwait**, **Bahrain** (an island), **Qatar** (a peninsula) and the **United Arab Emirates**. Cyprus, though in the eastern Mediterranean, is grouped with Europe on this site.

## The Caucasus (3)

Between the Black Sea and the Caspian Sea: **Georgia** in the north, **Armenia** in the southwest and **Azerbaijan** in the east, along the Caspian coast. Azerbaijan's Nakhchivan exclave sits separately on the other side of Armenia.

## Central Asia (5)

The five "-stans" north of Afghanistan:

1. **Kazakhstan**, by far the largest, and the largest landlocked country in the world.
2. **Uzbekistan**, in the middle, one of only two doubly landlocked countries.
3. **Turkmenistan**, to the southwest, on the Caspian Sea.
4. **Kyrgyzstan**, small and mountainous, to the east.
5. **Tajikistan**, south of Kyrgyzstan, bordering Afghanistan and China.

## South Asia (8)

**India** dominates, with **Pakistan** to the west and **Bangladesh** to the east. **Nepal** and **Bhutan** sit in the Himalayas between India and China; Nepal is the larger, western one. **Afghanistan** borders Pakistan and Iran. Off the coast are **Sri Lanka** and the **Maldives**, a chain of low coral islands.

## East Asia (5)

**China**, **Mongolia**, **North Korea**, **South Korea** and **Japan**. Mongolia is landlocked between China and Russia. Taiwan is drawn on the map but not asked, following the rule that the quiz covers UN member and observer states.

## Southeast Asia (11)

On the mainland: **Myanmar**, **Thailand**, **Laos**, **Cambodia** and **Vietnam**. Laos is the only landlocked country in Southeast Asia. The Malay Peninsula and islands hold **Malaysia**, **Singapore**, **Brunei**, **Indonesia**, the **Philippines** and **Timor-Leste**. Malaysia is split between the peninsula and northern Borneo, an island it shares with Brunei and Indonesia.

## Practice tips

- Learn the Gulf states as a sequence along the coast: Kuwait, Bahrain, Qatar, UAE, then Oman around the corner.
- For Central Asia, remember Kazakhstan's size and Uzbekistan's central position; the other three fit around them.
- Small countries such as Bahrain, Singapore and the Maldives appear as dots; on a phone, turn on Zoom.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many countries are in Asia?

This site counts 47. Totals vary depending on whether Russia, Turkey, Cyprus and the Caucasus countries are placed in Asia or Europe.

### What is the largest country in Asia?

China is the largest country entirely in Asia. Russia is larger but spans Europe and Asia.

### Which Asian countries are landlocked?

Twelve in this quiz: Afghanistan, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Bhutan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Laos, Mongolia, Nepal, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan and Uzbekistan. Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan and Turkmenistan border the Caspian Sea, but because it is a lake with no outlet to the ocean, they still count as landlocked.
